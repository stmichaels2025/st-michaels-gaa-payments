❓ Frequently Asked Questions

When is the draw held?

The draw is held weekly at St Michael’s pitch in Ballintogher.

How do the bonus draws work? ⭐

All auto renewal subscriptions are eligible to be entered into 4 bonus draws throughout the year, provided the subscription is active at the time of each bonus draw.

How do I cancel an auto renewal?

To cancel an auto renewal, please contact treasurer.stmichaels.sligo@gaa.ie.

Will I get a receipt?

For receipts, please contact treasurer.stmichaels.sligo@gaa.ie.

Who do I contact if there’s an issue?

Please contact any member of the executive committee or email treasurer.stmichaels.sligo@gaa.ie.
